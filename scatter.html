Chart 1.2 — all four on one axis: caliber vs split

The disciplines cannot share a raw x-axis — one is measured in kilometres covered and three in hours taken — so this one plots each performance as a percentage of the best mark in this dataset for its own discipline and sex. 100% is that dataset-best; right = closer to the ceiling. It is the only honest way to ask the paper's second question across all four at once: do the very best performances pace more evenly, and does the answer depend on how long the race is? Two things to read carefully: the extreme positive splits at low caliber are largely early-stoppers rather than faders, and the marathon field is compressed into the top of the caliber range because its per-sex cutoff (2:12 / 2:25) is narrow relative to the best mark — box-zoom into 88–100 to compare like with like.
