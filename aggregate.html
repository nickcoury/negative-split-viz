The whole field, sliced various ways. "24h halves" = the 24-hour split; "in-race 100mi" = the embedded 100-mile effort inside a 24h race (the green-line cohort). Charts 2–7 are 24-hour only; charts 0 and 1 are the cross-discipline comparison.

0 · The rate, by discipline — how pacing changes with duration

The share of every qualifying performance that ran a negative or an even second half, per discipline and sex. This is the project's premise as a measurement rather than an assertion: the negative split is ordinary in the marathon and close to extinct over 24 hours. One caveat that cuts in the safe direction — the ultras' halfway points are snapped to the last complete lap and biased negative, while the marathon's are measured on a surveyed mat, so the gap shown here is if anything understated.
