Exploration 12 · Pacing archetypes & replay

The shape of the race as a fingerprint. Cluster the rich-lap 24-hour field into archetypes, compare the mean shape of men vs women, and replay any single race against its even-pace ghost.

12.1 — Two-dimensional pacing archetypes

X = how much faster than average the first half ran (right = went out hard). Y = late surge (final-10% pace minus the mid-race pace; up = kicked at the end). Marker size = pace steadiness (bigger = more variable). The four corners name themselves.

12.2 — Mean pace shape, men vs women

Average cumulative position relative to an even pace, across the rich cohort, by sex. Above 0 = ahead of even pace at that point; the line ending back near 0 is a dead-even race. Does one sex, on average, front-load more?

12.3 — Race replay

Pick a race and press play. The line traces cumulative distance ahead of (above) / behind (below) an even pace as the race unfolds — the flat grey line at 0 is the even-pace "ghost". A negative split dips below, then climbs back through the ghost.
