Exploration 2 · Foresight — projecting from your pace so far

Foresight = your average pace from the start up to each point, projected to a finish (what you are "on pace" for, knowing only the race so far). Its mirror — average pace from each point to the end (the "green line") — is on the Hindsight page.
Read the intuitive way: the dotted gold line is your average pace (your actual result); above it = ahead of / faster than that average; below = behind it. A negative split sits below the line for most of the race (banking pace conservatively) then rises to meet it — e.g. Coury's DS American Record climbs from ~171 mi up to 173.0. Chart 2.2 shows the real projected value (24-hour: projected distance in miles; 100-mile: projected finish time, axis reversed so up = faster). The overlay (2.1) instead plots every race relative to its own average (dotted line at 0 = average). X: 24-hour = elapsed hours, 100-mile = miles covered. On mobile: pinch to zoom, or use ↔ Wide mode to drag across a bigger version.
