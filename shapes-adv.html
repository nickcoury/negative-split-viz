Exploration 4 · Shape metrics — full rich-lap cohort

Derived from every 24-hour result with full lap data (n≈705, all split classes). "% faster" = faster than that race's own average pace (up = faster). This is where even/negative shapes contrast with the positive majority.

1 · First-half vs second-half pace

Above the diagonal = negative split (2nd half faster). The even/neg points hug or cross the line; positives sit far below.

2 · Late surge vs split

Surge = final-10% pace minus middle-50% pace (% faster). Negative splitters have a real kick; faders go negative.

3 · Steadiness vs split

Steadiness = std-dev of segment pace (lower = smoother). Are even splits also steadier, or just balanced end-to-end?

4 · Average shape by split class (quarter by quarter)

Mean % faster-than-avg in each race quarter, per class. The signature curves.
