The ladder · how close to the record, and which way the split goes

One panel per discipline, shortest first, stacked — scroll to compare. X is the same quantity in every panel: how far off the benchmark this run was (1.00 = the benchmark, 1.10 = ten percent slower). Y is the split: below zero is a negative split. The panels are deliberately not overlaid — the question is how the cloud MOVES as the race gets longer, and 15,000 points on one axis answer it worse than ten small multiples do.

The benchmark is not the same KIND of number in every panel, and each panel says which it is using. Eight rungs are measured against the ratified world record for that sex, and for those the population cutoff — 110% of the record — is literally the right-hand edge of the data. The 100-mile and the 24-hour have no ratified record in constants.WORLD_RECORDS (their cutoffs are absolute: finish inside a day, cover 100 miles), so their benchmark is the best mark in this dataset and is labelled dataset best. They are the two panels whose x axis means something slightly different from the other eight.

The selection rule differs by rung too, and it is printed in every panel header (constants.cutoff_description()). These are not the same kind of bar: below the marathon it is 110% of the world record, the marathon and 100 km are hand-set, and the two longest are absolute. A rate read across panels is reading across three selection rules.

A rate is a knife edge at the short end. The negative-or-even percentage in each header is a count on one side of a fixed line at +1.0%, and how far it moves per 1 pp of field-wide shift runs from 35 pp at 10 km to 0.5 pp at 24 hours — a factor of seventy (analysis/rate_fragility.py). That is why the median is printed beside it: the median is monotone across the whole ladder and needs no threshold.

Format is confounded with duration below the 10 km. Every 800 m, 1500 m, mile and 5 km row here is a championship or invitational TRACK race — sit-and-kick, tactical, won on the last lap — while the 10 km, half and marathon rows are paced road time trials. So the short end of this ladder is not only shorter, it is a different KIND of race, and the panels cannot separate the two.
