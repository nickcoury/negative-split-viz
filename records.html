Exploration 13 · Record-attempt pacing

Do the very best performances — the ones near the ceiling of what's been run — pace unusually evenly? "Caliber" here is each performance as a % of the best mark in the dataset for its discipline and sex (a within-dataset record proxy; we don't hard-code official records).

13.1 — Caliber vs split

X = caliber (100% = the best mark in the dataset for that sex/discipline). Drag the slider to spotlight the record-chases; the running line is the mean split in a sliding caliber window. 24-hour = farther is better; 100-mile = faster is better.
