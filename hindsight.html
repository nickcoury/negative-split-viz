Exploration 3 · Hindsight / Green Line — projecting from your pace to the end

Hindsight (the "green line") = your average pace from each point to the end, projected to a finish — what your remaining pace was worth. Its mirror, pace from the start, is Foresight.
Read the intuitive way: the dotted gold line is your average pace (your actual result); above it = the rest of your race was faster than that average; below = slower. A negative split starts on the line and climbs above it toward the finish (you closed faster than your average) — e.g. Coury's DS American Record rises from 173.0 toward a ~188 mi/24h closing rate. Chart 3.2 shows the real projected value (24-hour: projected distance in miles; 100-mile: projected finish time, axis reversed so up = faster). The overlay (3.1) instead plots every race relative to its own average (dotted line at 0 = average). X: 24-hour = elapsed hours, 100-mile = miles covered. Hindsight is noisy near the very end (tiny remaining sample) — the mirror of Foresight's noisy start. On mobile: pinch to zoom, or use ↔ Wide mode.
